# Rome Conquest

A simple strategy game (more like simple POC to explore simple mechanics) where you lead Rome to conquer.

## Features

*   **Strategy Map**: View Europe, manage your provinces, and choose who to attack.
*   **Economy**: Collect taxes to fund your war machine. Manage happiness to prevent revolts.
*   **Recruitment**: Train legions to build up your army.
*   **Tactical Battles**: Watch your legions fight in real-time battles using formations.
*   **AI Opponents**: Rival factions (Barbarians, Carthage, Egypt) will recruit and try to conquer you.
*   **Win/Loss**: Conquer all provinces to win. Lose all provinces to suffer defeat.

## How to Play

1.  **Select a Province**: Click on a province to see its stats.
2.  **Recruit**: If you own it, click "Recruit" to add soldiers.
3.  **Attack**: Click "Conquer" to attack a neighbor. You need a neighboring army to invade.
4.  **End Turn**: Click "End Turn" to collect taxes and let the AI move.
5.  **Tax**: Click the Tax label (Top Right) to change tax rates. High tax gives more money but lowers happiness.

Made with Three.js and TypeScript.
